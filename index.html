About Kvali

"Kvali" means a trace

The trace a good dinner leaves: the smell of bread, a toast you remember, a recipe you want to try at home. Our chef grew up in Adjara and cooks from her grandmother's notebook, with fish from the morning market and wine from small family cellars.

Every dish on the menu lists its allergens, and the staff will tell you what can be made without nuts or dairy. Ask, we like questions.
